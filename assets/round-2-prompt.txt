Implement these clue types exactly:
1. Exactly k neighbors of a specified hex are terrain T.
2. A specified hex’s row contains exactly k tiles of terrain T.
3. All terrain-T tiles form one connected region.
4. No two terrain-T tiles touch.
5. Hex A and hex B have the same terrain.
